We use your email address and message only to reply to you, based on our legitimate interest in providing support (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Google may process this data in the United States under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK and Swiss extensions, or under Standard Contractual Clauses. See Google's privacy policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We keep your messages only as long as needed to help you, and we delete them sooner if you ask. To delete your messages, see [Delete your data](../delete/).
