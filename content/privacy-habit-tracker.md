# Privacy Policy: our habit tracker app

**Effective:** {{GO_LIVE_DATE}}  
**Last updated:** {{GO_LIVE_DATE}}

This policy covers our habit tracker app (the "App") as it works today. If a future version adds features that collect new data, we'll update this policy and tell you in the App before they take effect.

Our habit tracker app (the "App") is made by LAGNIAPPE SOLUTIONS LLC ("we", "us"). We built it to keep your information on your phone. This policy explains what that means in practice.

## The short version

- No accounts. No sign-up in the App. The App itself never collects your email.
- Your habits stay on your device. We don't have a server that stores them.
- No ads. No analytics. No tracking across apps or websites.
- We don't sell or share your personal information.
- No crash reports. This version of the App doesn't send crash reports. If we add them later, we'll update this policy first.
- Purchases go through Apple or Google, with RevenueCat helping us check what you bought.

## 1. What we collect

Very little from the App, and none of it identifies you. The App doesn't ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or health records.

## 2. What stays on your device

The App saves your habits, check-ins, reminder times, and settings in a database on your device. It also keeps a few app notes there, such as when you first opened the App, when you last exported a backup, when we last asked for a review, and a saved copy of your Pro status.

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

## 3. Backups (export and import)

You can export a backup file from Settings. The App hands the file to your phone's share sheet, and you decide where it goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or email). Once you send it somewhere, that service's privacy policy applies. We never receive the file.

Backup files are plain text (JSON) and include your habit names and history. Keep them somewhere private.

Import reads a file you pick and adds it to the App on your device.

## 4. Reminders

Reminders are local notifications. Your phone schedules and shows them itself. No push server is involved, and we don't receive anything. A reminder shows the habit's name on your screen. The App asks for notification permission only when you first set a reminder. You can turn it off any time in your phone's settings.

## 5. Crash reports

This version of the App doesn't send crash reports. It has no crash-reporting service turned on, so if the App crashes, nothing about it is sent to us or to anyone else by the App.

Your phone has its own setting for sharing diagnostics with app makers (on iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › Share With App Developers; on Android: your device's usage & diagnostics setting). It's your choice, and you can change it any time. If it's on, Apple or Google may collect crash and performance data about the App and show it to us, as reports or statistics, in App Store Connect or the Google Play Console. We use it only to find and fix bugs. Apple and Google handle that data under their own privacy policies. If you test a pre-release version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports with us whatever that setting is.

If we add crash reporting later, we'll update this policy first.

## 6. Purchases (Apple, Google, RevenueCat)

If you buy Pro, Apple or Google handles the payment. We never see your card or billing details.

We use RevenueCat to confirm what you bought and restore it. RevenueCat gives your install an anonymous, random app user ID. We don't send your name, email, or other personal details to RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives purchase information from the store (such as product, price, transaction ID, and dates) along with basic device and app info needed to do its job.

- Apple privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## 7. App Store reviews

Now and then the App may ask your phone to show the standard "rate this app" prompt. Apple or Google shows it. We don't receive your rating, beyond any public review you choose to post.

## 8. No tracking, no sale

We don't use advertising IDs. We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites. We don't sell or "share" personal information as California law defines those terms, and we haven't in the past 12 months. We don't use your data to build profiles or for targeted ads.

## 9. Children

The App isn't directed to children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU, EEA, UK, and Switzerland, or the minimum age where you live). If you think a child has given us their email, contact admin@lagniappeapps.com and we'll delete it.

## 10. Deleting your data

Your data is on your device, so you control it.

- Delete single habits in the App, or
- uninstall the App. That removes its database from your device.

Backup files you exported live wherever you saved them. Delete them there. Uninstalling doesn't cancel a subscription; see the Terms of Use for how.

We don't hold your habit data, so we can't delete or recover it for you. Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide and restore your purchases, and as their policies and the law require.

## 11. Your rights (GDPR, CCPA, and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- know what personal information we have about you,
- get a copy of it, including in a portable format,
- correct it,
- delete it,
- object to or limit how it's used,
- not be sold to or tracked (we don't do either),
- not be treated differently for using these rights,
- complain to your local data protection authority.

In practice, for App users we hold almost nothing that identifies you. If you'd like to ask anyway, email us. We won't treat you differently for asking.

**For people in the EU, EEA, UK, and Switzerland:** Where GDPR, the UK GDPR, or the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection applies, we process purchase data to provide what you bought (performance of a contract). Our provider RevenueCat processes data in the United States under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and, for Swiss data, the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework) or Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If the App starts collecting something new, we'll say so clearly before it does. For material changes, we'll tell you in the App before they take effect.
