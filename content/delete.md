# Delete your data: Checkalong (by Lagniappe Solutions LLC)

**Checkalong doesn't have accounts.** Your habits, check-ins, reminders and settings are stored only on your phone. We don't have a copy.

## To delete your Checkalong data

1. In the app, delete individual habits; or
2. **Uninstall the app.** This removes all of its data from your phone.
3. If you exported backup files, delete them wherever you saved them (Files, Drive, email…).

Uninstalling doesn't cancel a Pro subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
