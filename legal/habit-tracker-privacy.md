# [APP NAME] Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** TBD at publish

[APP NAME] is made by LAGNIAPPE SOLUTIONS LLC ("we", "us"). We built it to keep your information on your phone. This policy explains what that means in practice.

## The short version

- No accounts. No sign-up in the App. The App itself never collects your email.
- Your habits stay on your device. We don't have a server that stores them.
- No ads. No analytics. No tracking across apps or websites.
- We don't sell or share your personal information.
- Crash reports are optional. In the EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland, and related territories (including EU overseas regions and territories, Gibraltar, the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man, the Faroe Islands, Greenland, and Svalbard), they start off. They also start off if your phone doesn't report a country or region. Elsewhere they start on. If they're on, a crash sends a technical report with your habit text removed. You can turn them off any time in Settings > Privacy.
- Purchases go through Apple or Google, with RevenueCat helping us check what you bought.
- If you join the launch waitlist on our website, we keep your email only to tell you when the App launches. See [Website and launch waitlist](#9-website-and-launch-waitlist).

## 1. What we collect

Very little, and none of it identifies you. The App doesn't ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or health records.

If you sign up for our launch waitlist on our website (not in the App), we collect your email as described in [Website and launch waitlist](#9-website-and-launch-waitlist).

## 2. What stays on your device

The App saves your habits, check-ins, reminder times, and settings in a database on your device. It also keeps a few app notes there, such as when you first opened the App, when you last exported a backup, when we last asked for a review, and a saved copy of your Pro status.

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

## 3. Backups (export and import)

You can export a backup file from Settings. The App hands the file to your phone's share sheet, and you decide where it goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or email). Once you send it somewhere, that service's privacy policy applies. We never receive the file.

Backup files are plain text (JSON) and include your habit names and history. Keep them somewhere private.

Import reads a file you pick and adds it to the App on your device.

## 4. Reminders

Reminders are local notifications. Your phone schedules and shows them itself. No push server is involved, and we don't receive anything. A reminder shows the habit's name on your screen. The App asks for notification permission only when you first set a reminder. You can turn it off any time in your phone's settings.

## 5. Crash reports (Sentry)

To fix bugs, the App can send crash and error reports to Sentry, a crash-reporting service. This only happens in releases where we've turned it on, and only while "Send crash reports" is on in the App.

What a report can include: the error and where in the code it happened, the app version, device model, operating system version, and the screen route (such as "habit/:id/edit").

What we remove before it is sent:

- your habit names and "tiny version" text,
- file paths on your device,
- user and network-request details,
- console logs, and the text of anything you tapped.

We don't turn on Sentry's performance tracing, session tracking, screenshots, or screen-layout capture. Crash reports aren't linked to you and aren't used for advertising. Sentry is set not to store your IP address. **Crash reports are optional.** In the EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland, and related territories (including EU overseas regions and territories, Gibraltar, the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man, the Faroe Islands, Greenland, and Svalbard), "Send crash reports" starts **off** and we only send reports if you turn it on. It also starts off if your phone doesn't report a country or region. Elsewhere it starts on. We go by the region in your phone's settings, read on your phone, not by your location. You can change it any time in **Settings > Privacy**. When it's off:

- the crash-reporting service isn't started the next time you open the App, and it's shut down right away if it's running,
- any report that hasn't been sent yet is dropped, and
- your choice is saved on your device only. It isn't included in backups.

You can turn crash reports back on at any time from the same switch.

Sentry privacy policy: https://sentry.io/privacy/

## 6. Purchases (Apple, Google, RevenueCat)

If you buy Pro, Apple or Google handles the payment. We never see your card or billing details.

We use RevenueCat to confirm what you bought and restore it. RevenueCat gives your install an anonymous, random app user ID. We don't send your name, email, or other personal details to RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives purchase information from the store (such as product, price, transaction ID, and dates) along with basic device and app info needed to do its job.

- Apple privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## 7. App Store reviews

Now and then the App may ask your phone to show the standard "rate this app" prompt. Apple or Google shows it. We don't receive your rating, beyond any public review you choose to post.

## 8. No tracking, no sale

We don't use advertising IDs. We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites. We don't sell or "share" personal information as California law defines those terms, and we haven't in the past 12 months. We don't use your data to build profiles or for targeted ads.

## 9. Website and launch waitlist

If you join our launch waitlist on our website, we collect your email address and the date you signed up. We also store a scrambled (hashed) copy of your email to prevent duplicate sign-ups. That's all. We don't store your IP address with your sign-up, and our website uses no cookies or analytics. Like any website, our hosting provider (Microsoft Azure) processes your IP address briefly to deliver the page and protect the service.

This is separate from the App.

- **Why:** only to tell you when the App launches. We never sell or share your email for any other purpose.
- **Where:** Microsoft Azure Table Storage in the East US 2 region, which Microsoft runs for us as our service provider. The launch email is sent through **Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France)**, which acts as our processor and stores data in the European Union. Brevo privacy policy: https://www.brevo.com/legal/privacypolicy/
- **How long:** we delete the list 30 days after the launch email. If we never launch, we delete your email 12 months after you signed up.
- **Unsubscribe or delete:** use the unsubscribe link in the email, or write to admin@lagniappeapps.com and we'll delete your address within 30 days.
- **Your choices:** you can ask to see, correct, or delete your email at any time. We rely on your consent, and you can withdraw it whenever you like.

## 10. Children

The App isn't directed to children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU, EEA, and UK, or the minimum age where you live). The website waitlist is for people 16 and older. If you think a child has given us their email, contact admin@lagniappeapps.com and we'll delete it.

## 11. Deleting your data

Your data is on your device, so you control it.

- Delete single habits in the App, or
- uninstall the App. That removes its database from your device.

Backup files you exported live wherever you saved them. Delete them there. Uninstalling doesn't cancel a subscription; see the Terms of Use for how.

We don't hold your habit data, so we can't delete or recover it for you. Crash reports, if any, are kept by Sentry for a limited time under its retention settings. Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide and restore your purchases, and as their policies and the law require.

## 12. Your rights (GDPR, CCPA, and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- know what personal information we have about you,
- get a copy of it, including in a portable format,
- correct it,
- delete it,
- object to or limit how it's used,
- not be sold to or tracked (we don't do either),
- not be treated differently for using these rights,
- complain to your local data protection authority.

In practice, for App users we hold almost nothing that identifies you. If you joined the waitlist, we hold your email as described above. If you'd like to ask anyway, email us. We won't treat you differently for asking.

**For people in the EU, EEA, UK, and Switzerland:** Where GDPR, the UK GDPR, or the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection applies, we process crash reports based on your consent (you can withdraw it any time with the "Send crash reports" switch), and purchase data to provide what you bought (performance of a contract). Waitlist emails are processed based on your consent. Our providers Sentry, RevenueCat, and Microsoft process data in the United States under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and, for Swiss data, the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework) or Standard Contractual Clauses. Brevo stores waitlist data in the EU; where it or its subprocessors transfer data outside the EU, they rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or another lawful safeguard.
